# 🌸 SakhiLens AI

## HACKARENA-KIRTHICK

### Government schemes, made simple.

SakhiLens AI is an AI-powered accessibility platform designed to help women understand government schemes without requiring advanced digital knowledge.

---

## 💡 Idea

The user uploads a government scheme poster.

SakhiLens AI:

1. Reads the poster
2. Explains the scheme
3. Allows language selection
4. Identifies benefits
5. Checks eligibility
6. Generates a document checklist
7. Provides next steps

## ⭐ Features

### 📸 Image Upload

Users can upload:

- Government scheme posters
- Images
- PDFs

### 🌐 Multiple Languages

Supported demo languages:

- English
- Tamil
- Hindi
- Telugu
- Malayalam
- Kannada

### 🤖 AI Explanation

The AI converts complicated government language into simple language.

### ✅ Eligibility Checker

The system compares user information with scheme requirements.

### 📄 Document Checklist

Shows documents that may be required.

### 🛡️ Safety

The production system should verify official government sources and warn users about suspicious links, OTP requests or payment requests.
